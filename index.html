1. Overview

Host Invaders is an educational science channel that publishes illustrated, narrated videos about parasites to YouTube. This privacy policy explains how we handle information in connection with our app and content.

2. Data We Collect

Host Invaders does not collect, store, or sell personal information from viewers or subscribers. We do not operate a website with user accounts, forms, or tracking.

3. Platform APIs

Host Invaders uses the YouTube Data API solely to upload videos, set thumbnails, and post comments on our own YouTube channel. We access only the permissions required for those actions. We do not access, store, or process any data belonging to other users.

4. Third-Party Services

Content is produced with help from third-party services (Anthropic, Fal.ai, ElevenLabs). These services are governed by their own privacy policies. No viewer data is shared with these services.

5. Cookies

This website does not use cookies or any tracking technology.

6. Children's Privacy

Host Invaders content is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
